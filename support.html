Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open For Parents → Restore purchase. Purchases are handled by Apple and work with Family Sharing.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The game is fully offline. Only the App Store purchase and restore need a network connection.

How do I change the language?

Clefscape follows your device language: English by default, Simplified Chinese when your device is set to Chinese. There is no in-app language switch.

How do I reset progress?

Open For Parents, pass the grown-ups check, then choose Reset progress. This clears the active progress on this device. If you previously recovered an unreadable save, its recovery copy remains on the device; deleting the app removes its local data. Existing device backups are managed separately.

Is my child's data collected?

The app does not send us personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics SDKs, or tracking. If you email support, we receive your email address and message. See the privacy policy.

Clefscape is in development. You can contact us in English or Chinese.
