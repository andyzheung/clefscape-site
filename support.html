Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open For Parents → Restore purchase. Purchases are handled by Apple and work with Family Sharing.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The game is fully offline. Only the App Store purchase and restore need a network connection.

How do I change the language?

Clefscape follows your device language: English by default, Simplified Chinese when your device is set to Chinese. There is no in-app language switch.

How do I reset progress?

Open For Parents, pass the grown-ups check, then choose Reset progress. This deletes local progress on this device.

Is my child's data collected?

No. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
