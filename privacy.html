Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

HomeSupport

Short version

Clefscape is made for children and families. We designed it to collect nothing.

  • No account.
  • No ads, no ad networks, no tracking.
  • No analytics SDKs and no third-party data sharing.
  • Progress stays on your device.

What we collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, birthdays, locations, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers, or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

Level progress, stars, practice statistics, and settings are stored in the app's own folder on your device. They are not uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes this local data. A parent or guardian can also clear progress in the Parent Zone.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We receive only the entitlement status needed to unlock content; we do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the App Store transaction.

Children's privacy

Clefscape is intended for children ages 5–9 with a parent or guardian nearby. Because we collect no personal information, there is no server-side data to access or delete. A parent or guardian can clear all local progress on the device.

Network

The game is fully offline. The only network use is Apple's StoreKit for purchase and restore, plus opening the support or privacy page in your browser if you choose to.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Material changes will be reflected in a new app version.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue. Please do not include personal information about a child.
