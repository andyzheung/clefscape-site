Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

HomeSupport

Short version

Clefscape is made for children and families. The app does not send personal information to us.

  • No account.
  • No ads, no ad networks, no tracking.
  • No analytics SDKs and no third-party data sharing.
  • Progress is stored on your device and may be included in device backups.

What we collect

The app does not send us names, email addresses, phone numbers, birthdays, locations, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers, or other personal information. If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and the information in your message so we can respond.

What stays on your device

Level progress, stars, practice statistics, and settings are stored in the app's own folder on your device. The app does not upload them to us. They may be included in a device backup, depending on your Apple backup settings. A parent or guardian can clear active progress in the Parent Zone. If an unreadable save was previously recovered, its recovery copy remains on the device. Deleting the app deletes its local data; existing backups are managed separately.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The app receives transaction information needed to check access; we do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the App Store transaction.

Children's privacy

Clefscape is intended for children ages 5–9 with a parent or guardian nearby. The app does not send us personal information. A parent or guardian can clear local progress on the device. If a parent or guardian contacts us by email, we receive their email address and the contents of that message.

Network

The game is fully offline. The only network use is Apple's StoreKit for purchase and restore, plus opening the support or privacy page in your browser if you choose to.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Material changes will be reflected in a new app version.

Contact

Questions or concerns: email andyzheung@aliyun.com. Please do not include personal information about a child.
