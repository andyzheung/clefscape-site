What is Clefscape?

Clefscape turns the five-line staff into a running track. Guide Dot up and down to catch each note — every note you catch plays the real melody. Every few notes, a Piano Gate asks you to name what you just caught on a small keyboard.

It is not a rhythm game, and it is not a piano lesson. It is sight-reading practice that feels like play.
